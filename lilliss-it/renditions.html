Renditions

Four takes on the same page. Identical copy, identical facts — four different ideas about what a one-person IT business should feel like. They come from the trade rather than from a moodboard: a docket book, a patch panel, a venue after close.

The Unbroken LineNight. One cable leaves the lit window and runs unbroken to a person. DocketThe job docket book. Manila and carbon blue, typewriter, stamped. RackA patch panel elevation. Slate, label-maker, colour-coded runs. After CloseThe venue once the lights are down. Deep green, brass, one warm bulb.
Every rendition carries the same verified copy, byte for byte: same services, same certifications, same ABN, same “trading since 2019, registered 2026”. No percentages, no testimonials, no client counts. Only the point of view changes.
