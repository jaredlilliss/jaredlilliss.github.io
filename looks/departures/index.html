/queue-board/

Running

Aussie Health Utility

A collector reads how many people are waiting in each NSW emergency department that publishes a live count. It reads every fifteen minutes and appends the reading to a PostgreSQL series that is never rewritten.

Fifty-nine departments publish a live count. NSW has 176 in total, so this is the fifty-nine, not the state. Collecting started on 20 July 2026. There are now more than 120,000 readings.

The feed publishes a count of people waiting, not a duration. Nothing on these pages is a waiting time.

No personal or health information is collected, because the schema has no fields for it.

The live board is at /queue-board/. The Tally is at /waits/.

/record/

Running · one row closed

The collection record

The upstream feed publishes the current moment only and keeps no history. A reading not taken at the time is gone for good.

It ran on a laptop first. The laptop slept, so the early weeks have gaps. Since 23 August 2026 it has run on a small Melbourne server, and nothing after that date is missing.

A database dump is taken at 03:30 Melbourne time each night and copied off the machine.

The full record is at /record/. The source is at github.com/jaredlilliss/aussie-health-utility, a public mirror; the working repository stays private.

Concept

The Span

A design concept. One screen holding 1975 to 2117: the space programme of the United Arab Emirates drawn as a single span you travel by dragging.

Fifteen milestones, each a drawn line that means its own event rather than an icon off a shelf. Every one names the source that establishes it, and thirteen link straight to the government page they came from. Nothing is estimated.

Deep-linkable to any year, keyboard-navigable, and readable as a plain sourced list with scripting switched off. An unsolicited concept, no affiliation.

Live at lilliss.au/xperience-concept/spine.html.
