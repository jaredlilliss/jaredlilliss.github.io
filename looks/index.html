The looks

Two alternative designs for this site, and one piece that is not competing with them. Same facts, same links, one self-contained file each.

None of them is live. The site itself is unchanged.

Two front pages — pick one

  1. 01The Rail

    The work along one line, 2019 to now. Drag, scroll or use the arrows; the ground colour is where you are in time, and each stop carries a drawn mark that means its own event.

  2. 02The Section

    A cut downward through the layers the work actually touches, from the terminal on a counter to the row in a database and back up to a public page. Depth is the scroll, so unlike the Rail it works on a phone.

Not a candidate

  1. 03Every Reading

    The whole collection record drawn as one surface — 59 emergency departments down, every day across, in a hand-written shader. It is deliberately heavy and built for a mouse, which is why it could never be the front door: a venue owner whose tills have just died should never wait on a graphics card. It reads its numbers live, so nothing on it can go stale, and where the collector was not running the material is simply not there. If a machine cannot run it, it says so and draws the flat version instead.
