View 02 · Detail A Aussie Health Utility

A collector that reads how many people are waiting in each NSW emergency department that publishes a live count, every fifteen minutes, and appends the reading to a PostgreSQL series that is never rewritten.

It has been collecting since 20 July 2026 and holds more than 120,000 readings. No personal or health information is collected, because the schema has no fields for it.

It ran on a laptop first, which slept, so the early weeks have gaps. Since 23 August 2026 it has run on a small Melbourne server and nothing after that is missing. A database dump is taken at 03:30 Melbourne time each night and copied off the machine.

View 04 · Detail C The Span

A design concept. One screen holding 1975 to 2117: the space programme of the United Arab Emirates drawn as a single span you travel by dragging.

Fifteen milestones, each a drawn line that means its own event rather than an icon off a shelf, and every one names the source that establishes it, thirteen linking straight to the government page it came from. Nothing is estimated.

It is deep-linkable to any year, keyboard-navigable, and readable as a plain sourced list with scripting switched off. An unsolicited concept, no affiliation. Live at lilliss.au/xperience-concept/spine.html.
