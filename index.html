Aussie Health Utility

PythonPostgreSQLLinux VPScronrclone

A privacy-by-design data platform over public Australian health datasets: a polling loop feeding an append-only PostgreSQL time series of live patients-waiting counts for NSW emergency departments, with the privacy posture set at the schema level — no personal or health information is ever collected. Runs unattended on a Melbourne VPS with health-checked collection, daily dumps, and automated offsite backups. Tens of thousands of readings and growing.

The Span — design concept

Single fileInteractiveZero libraries

One screen holding 1975 to 2117: the UAE’s space programme drawn as a single span you travel by dragging. Fifteen milestones, each a drawn line that means its own event rather than an icon off a shelf — and every one names the source that establishes it, thirteen linking straight to the government page it came from. Nothing is estimated. Deep-linkable to any year, keyboard-navigable, and readable as a plain sourced list with scripting switched off.
