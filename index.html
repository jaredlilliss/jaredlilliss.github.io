Current project

Aussie Health Utility

In development

A free consumer health information app for Australians. It presents public, aggregate healthcare data in one place:

  • Live emergency department wait times (NSW)
  • Pharmacy opening hours and open/closed status
  • Medicare Benefits Schedule information and typical specialist costs

The app is anonymous by design: no user accounts, no advertising, no tracking, and no personal information collected. All displayed data is drawn from public government sources and attributed to them.

Aussie Health Utility is an information tool only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Always consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

How we work with data sources

Our services retrieve only published, public, aggregate data. Automated access identifies itself honestly (a contactable User-Agent on every request), respects rate limits, and follows each source's licensing — including seeking written permission where a licence requires it.
