From 20 Jul 2026 · NSW emergency departments · every 15 minutes

  • Not a waiting time. The feed publishes how many people are waiting for treatment. There is no minutes field in it.
  • Not statewide. 59 emergency departments publish a live count. NSW has 176.

Every fifteen minutes since 20 July 2026 a collector asks NSW Health how many people are waiting for treatment in each emergency department that publishes a live count, and writes the answer down. Nothing is ever overwritten.

The picture on this page is that record, drawn. It needs JavaScript and a canvas, so here it is in words: until 23 August the collector ran on a laptop that Windows suspended whenever the machine idled, and five whole days recorded nothing at all (26–29 July and 1 August) while four more caught a single reading. On 23 August it moved to a Melbourne server. Both collectors ran on the day of the move, so that day holds more readings than the 96 a single collector can take.

The gaps are permanent. The upstream feed serves current state only — there is no historical endpoint — so a reading that was missed cannot be recovered, ever. That is why the collector was built before anything you could look at.
