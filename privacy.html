Who we are

One person.

Jared Lilliss, trading as Lilliss IT (ABN 78 576 434 613), of New South Wales, Australia — referred to below as “we”.

This policy applies to the Lilliss IT website (at lilliss.au, and at jaredlilliss.github.io while that domain is being set up) and to the Aussie Health Utility app.

What this website collects

Nothing.

No cookies, no analytics, no tracking of any kind. We do not collect personal information from visitors.

One third party sees a request when you load this page, and it reports nothing back to us:

  • Our host, GitHub Pages, automatically logs the IP address of each visit and stores it on its own systems for security purposes — see GitHub’s data collection statement and Privacy Statement. We do not receive or have access to these logs.
  • Nothing else. This site loads no fonts, scripts, stylesheets, images or beacons from any other domain — the typefaces are served from this site itself. You can verify that in your browser’s network tab: every request should point at this domain and nowhere else.

What the app collects

Nothing.

Aussie Health Utility has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. It does not collect, store or transmit personal information.

What it displays — live patients-waiting counts for emergency departments, pharmacy opening hours, and Medicare Benefits Schedule cost information — is public, aggregate data published by government sources.

If you email us

Only what you send.

We receive your address and your message. We use it only to reply, and keep it only as ordinary correspondence.

What we never do

Sell, share, market, profile.

  • We do not sell, share or disclose personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use personal information for marketing.
  • We do not use personal information in automated decision-making.
  • We hold no databases of personal information at all. Because none is collected, none is retained, and there is nothing to delete.

Overseas disclosure

None by us.

We do not disclose personal information to overseas recipients.

The one qualification: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure is in the United States. So the server logs described above are processed and held there by GitHub — not by us. Nothing else about this site leaves the country on our account: the fonts are served from this site rather than from Google, so no font request is made and no third party sees your address because of one.

How information is held

One mailbox.

The only personal information we hold is email correspondence. It is kept in a professionally hosted, access-controlled mailbox protected by multi-factor authentication.

Access and deletion

Ask and it is gone.

Because we hold no personal information beyond any email correspondence you have sent us, there is usually nothing to access or correct.

If you would like correspondence from you deleted, email us and we will delete it.

Questions and complaints

Within 30 days.

Privacy questions or complaints: jaredlilliss@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes to this policy

Before, not after.

If our practices ever change — for example, if a future version of the app introduces a feature that handles personal information — we will update this policy before that change takes effect and note the new effective date here.
