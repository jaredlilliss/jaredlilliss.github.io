Privacy Policy

Lilliss IT · Effective August 2026

The short version: we do not collect personal information. This website has no analytics, no cookies and no tracking, and the Aussie Health Utility app has no accounts, no advertising and collects nothing about you.

Who we are

This policy covers Jared Lilliss, trading as Lilliss IT (ABN 78 576 434 613), of New South Wales, Australia — referred to below as "we" — and applies to this website (lilliss.au) and the Aussie Health Utility app.

What we collect

This website: we do not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind, and we do not collect personal information. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, automatically logs the IP address of each visit and stores it on its own systems for security purposes (see GitHub's data collection statement and Privacy Statement). We do not receive or have access to these logs. The page fonts are served by Google Fonts; when your browser fetches them, Google receives the request (including your IP address) under its Google Fonts privacy notice. We do not receive any of that information.

The app: nothing. Aussie Health Utility has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics. It does not collect, store or transmit personal information. The information it displays — emergency department wait times, pharmacy opening hours, and Medicare Benefits Schedule cost information — is public, aggregate data published by government sources.

Email: if you email us, we receive what you send (your address and your message). We use it only to reply and keep it only as ordinary correspondence.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, share or disclose personal information to anyone.
  • We do not use personal information for marketing.
  • We do not use personal information in automated decision-making.
  • We hold no databases of personal information at all. Because no personal information is collected, none is retained and there is nothing to delete.

Overseas disclosure

We do not disclose personal information to overseas recipients. The one qualification: this website is hosted on GitHub Pages and its fonts are served by Google Fonts, both of whose infrastructure is located in the United States — so the server and font-request logs described above are processed and held there by GitHub and Google respectively, not by us.

How information is held

The only personal information we hold is email correspondence, which is kept in a professionally hosted, access-controlled mailbox protected by multi-factor authentication.

Access and correction

Because we hold no personal information beyond any email correspondence you have sent us, there is usually nothing to access or correct. If you would like correspondence from you deleted, email us and we will delete it.

Questions and complaints

Privacy questions or complaints: jaredlilliss@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

If our practices ever change — for example, if a future version of the app introduces a feature that handles any personal information — we will update this policy before that change takes effect and note the new effective date here.
